Operating Expense Compositionⓘ

Mission Spending & Distributionsⓘ

Charitable-purpose disbursements (Part I col. d) and qualifying distributions (Part XII) are what the Trust actually spent on its mission. Minimum investment return (Part X) is a legally required formula — roughly 5% of investment assets — not the Trust's actual investment performance; see the chart below for how much above or below that floor each measure landed.
Surplus over minimum investment return — positive bars mean the Trust spent more on its mission than the legal floor required; negative bars mean less (which is not a violation: as a private operating foundation, the Getty is exempt from the standard payout test this floor implies for ordinary foundations).

People Costsⓘ

Salaries & wages, pension/benefits, and officer/trustee compensation (Part I lines 13–15) — three separate line items on the form, stacked here to show total workforce cost. Officer/trustee compensation is reported apart from "other employee" salaries, not included within them.
